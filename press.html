Press Kit

Press & Media

Toronto's Odonis Odonis — Constantin Tzenos and Denholm Whale — released their self-titled sixth album November 14, 2025 on After Hours, in partnership with Royal Mountain Records. Nine tracks reconnecting with the alt-rock touchstones that shaped them: The Cure circa Disintegration, New Order's Power, Corruption & Lies era, and the 1987–92 Creation Records roster.

Fact Sheet

Quick Facts

Artist: Odonis Odonis

Members: Constantin Tzenos, Denholm Whale

Based in: Toronto, Ontario, Canada

Formed: 2009

Genre: Industrial / Post-Punk / Cold Wave

Latest Album: Odonis Odonis (self-titled), released November 14, 2025

Label: After Hours (released in partnership with Royal Mountain Records)

Vinyl: Translucent Black Ice, limited to 500 copies

Latest Singles: Hijacked (88 Remix); Yeah Whatever (Reinterpreted) feat. DJ Dwight Hybrid

Prior Albums: Hollandaze (2011), Hard Boiled Soft Boiled (2014), Post Plague (2016, Polaris Music Prize longlist), No Pop (2017), Reaction EP (2019, opens with "Collector"), Spectrums (2021), ICON EP (2023, rights reclaimed)

Booking (Europe): Heinz — Heinz@rowzero.agency (Row Zero Agency)

Booking (US): Jack — jack@rockyroadtouring.com (Rocky Road Touring)
